# Innovative Internal Test checklist

## Before submission

- [ ] Keep the report focused on CommerceLens and its methods, results, and limitations.
- [ ] Keep team and institutional details on the presentation cover only.
- [ ] Check the report and slides against the current Innovative Internal Test rubric and department instructions.
- [ ] Read the methods and results and be ready to explain the revenue calculation, fixed RFM rules, and category-pair counts.
- [ ] Keep the synthetic-data disclosure visible. The bundled records and metrics are generated for demonstration and are not real store results.
- [ ] Use only screenshots and evaluation results that were actually produced.
- [ ] Open `index.html` in a browser and review the dashboard, filters, CSV import, and CSV export.
- [ ] Check that the final package contains the application, report, presentation, workbook, CSV, and project guide.
- [ ] If a live demo link is required, follow GITHUB_VERCEL_DEPLOYMENT.md and confirm the published page opens.

## Project scope

CommerceLens is a descriptive analytics prototype. Its RFM groups use fixed rules; category co-occurrence does not establish causation; and the bundled dataset is illustrative. The prototype does not include authentication, a database, checkout, forecasting, taxes, shipping, refunds, or cost-of-goods analysis.
